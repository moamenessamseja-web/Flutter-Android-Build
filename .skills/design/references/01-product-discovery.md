# Product Discovery

Understand the job before styling the screen.

Capture: product promise; primary users and real context; top 1–3 jobs; task frequency and failure cost; device/environment; online/offline assumptions; data sensitivity; constraints (architecture, screen count, SDK, time, identity); and evidence sources (owner brief, code, screenshots, feedback).

Tag findings as **Fact** (directly stated/visible), **Observation** (directly seen), **Inference** (plausible, with rationale), or **Unknown**. Do not invent demographic personas or market research. Prefer: “When [context], the user needs to [task] so they can [outcome], despite [constraint].”

Rank tasks by importance, frequency, and cost of failure. The home screen should privilege the most important current task/state, not every feature. Use PRODUCT-DESIGN-BRIEF.md; update existing briefs instead of duplicating them.
